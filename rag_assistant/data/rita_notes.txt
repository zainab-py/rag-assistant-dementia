Patient Name: Rita Sharma  
Age: 72  
Condition: Moderate-stage dementia  

Daily Behavior Patterns:
- Rita often becomes overwhelmed in noisy or crowded environments. She responds better in calm, familiar spaces with minimal sensory input.
- She forgets where rooms are, especially the bathroom. Visual cues like signs on doors help.
- She gets agitated if routines are suddenly changed. She prefers being told what’s about to happen in a calm voice.

Food Preferences:
- Rita dislikes spicy food and avoids onions and garlic.
- She prefers soft foods like khichdi, mashed potatoes, curd rice, or soft fruits like bananas.
- She enjoys sweet items, especially custard or fruit with honey.

Medication Notes:
- She consistently forgets to take her **afternoon medications** (around 2:00 PM). She rarely forgets the morning dose.
- Caregivers are advised to gently remind her using visual cues or by associating the dose with an activity like tea time.

Comfort & Triggers:
- She calms down when given a warm blanket or shown old family photos.
- Loud TV or arguments can cause her to retreat and become non-verbal.
- Music from her youth (old Hindi film songs) can lift her mood and reduce anxiety.

Physical Cues:
- When confused, she tends to wring her hands or look at the floor.
- If she looks around repeatedly or walks in circles, she may be trying to find something (usually the bathroom or a familiar object).

General Notes:
- Do not correct her harshly if she misremembers names or places. Instead, gently redirect or validate.
- She responds well to consistent faces and friendly tone.
- Never rush her — slowing down speech and actions makes her feel safer.
